Lately Elon Musk has recently been publicly talking about performance of Twitter. This article will talk about one important aspect of web performance, Time to First Byte (TTFB) and how changes have been made in this area to provide customers with a better experience. TTFB is a metric that measures the time between a clients request and the time for the first byte of the response.
