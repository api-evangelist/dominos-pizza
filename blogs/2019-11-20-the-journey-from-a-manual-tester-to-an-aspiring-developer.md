Sweat, Tears & Code I’ve been a manual tester for just over 5 years (3 of which have been at Domino’s), and since late last year I’ve been on a journey to learn testing automation tools such as SpecFlow in large projects. To understand how to use automation tools such as SpecFlow effectively – I’d need to learn development from the beginning. In the sense of ‘learn to walk before you can run’.
